# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in makr-code community spaces should feel welcome and be treated with respect.

- Be considerate, use clear and constructive language, and make room for different experiences.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Respect boundaries and accept good-faith feedback.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share someone's private information, or send unwanted sexual content.
- Do not disrupt discussions or retaliate against someone who raises a concern.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or exclude participants depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. They should consider context and protect the privacy of people involved.

For a non-sensitive concern, contact @makr-code through the community's public support issues. For sensitive reports, do not post personal information or incident details publicly. Ask @makr-code only for a private reporting channel, without details; if none is available, do not publish the sensitive report. For security vulnerabilities, follow [Security](SECURITY.md).
